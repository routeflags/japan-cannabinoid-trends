Key Findings

  • 13 cannabinoids tracked: 5 baseline (CBD, THC, CBN, CBG, THCV) + 8 emerging (HHC, THC-O, THCH, HHCH, CRDP, H4CBH, HHBD, CRDH)
  • 7 compounds now subject to specific Japanese regulation (THC, THCV, HHC, THC-O, THCH, HHCH, CBN)
  • JCSET-F001: HHCH shows post-regulation decline in search interest (temporal association)
  • Emergence Score system classifies compounds from 0 (not detected) to 4 (high-growth emerging)
  • 5 new cannabinoids emerged during 2022-2023, 2 more in 2025

Emergence Score System

The Emergence Score (ES) is a composite indicator of compound emergence intensity:

0
Not detected
No non-zero week in the collected window (not evidence that search volume is zero)
1
Sporadic detection
Isolated non-zero weeks, no sustained pattern
2
Sustained detection
Regular appearance but low growth rate
3
Rapid growth
Significant increase in search interest
4
High-growth emerging
Rapid growth + recent emergence + regulatory attention

Components: Growth (peak/mean ratio), Acceleration (rate of change), Persistence (non-zero weeks share), Novelty (recency of first mention).

Derived values: first mention is the first week with a non-zero relative value inside the collected 5-year window; peak week is the week with the largest relative value. The Emergence Score is a qualitative class, not a statistical estimate.

Data Summary

Compounds are classified by market status: baseline (established phytocannabinoids with a pre-existing market presence) or emerging (semi-synthetic or newly marketed cannabinoids that entered the Japanese market during the window). First-mention timing is reported as a separate field and is not the cohort criterion: HHC's first non-zero week (2021-11-28) precedes 2022 although HHC is an emerging compound.

Data Source

Google Trends (JP), one collection on 2026-10-02 with the timeframes today 5-y and today 12-m, collector google-trends-mcp. The legacy run records do not carry a collector version. Request terms are the compound names in the Data Summary table; this page uses no compare_terms request, so each compound's series is normalised to its own peak (100) and the values are not comparable between compounds.

The 5-year baseline for the established compounds was collected separately on 2026-10-06 (run 20261008T093228Z-google-trends-5y-baseline).

Regulatory Sources

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) official sources, including designated drug lists and press releases. The regulatory status shown is the status published in those sources as of 2026-10-02; this page reports it and does not make an independent legal determination.

Request window vs observed range. The request is the relative timeframe (today 5-y / today 12-m) resolved by the collector at collection time; the observed 5-year range is 2021-09-26 to 2026-10-03. The legacy records do not state an absolute request window, so the observed range must not be read as the request condition.

Derivation. First mention = the first week with a non-zero relative value in the collected 5-year series. Peak week = the week with the largest value. This page publishes no 5-year or 12-month average, and no non-zero-week count, so those statistics are not claimed here.

Value classes. First-mention, peak and Emergence Score figures are derived from the recorded summaries; regulatory dates and the run identifiers are observed from the cited MHLW and run records; interpretation is labelled separately in each finding card.

Retention. The weekly series behind the 2026-10-02 summary files were not retained, so the first-mention values cannot be recomputed from the repository. The values are reproduced from the recorded summaries; THCH has no 5-year run record and is reproduced from the research note. The withdrawn aggregate CSV is not used as a source.

Repeated collection. A later re-collection of the same compound terms (2026-10-14/23, today 5-y) retained the weekly series and observed different first non-zero weeks for some compounds (for example H4CBH 2022-12-04 and HHBD 2022-09-18 as isolated single-week values, THCH 2022-09-04, THCV 2022-02-27). v1.0 keeps the 2026-10-02 record; the later run is not substituted and its values are not combined with v1.0. Repeated collection is therefore known to change these dates and the stability of the first-mention series is not established.

Redistribution. The page shows aggregates, run identifiers and request conditions only. Google Trends raw payloads and the withdrawn CSV are not redistributed.

Limitations

  • Relative indices: Google Trends values (0-100) are relative, not absolute search volumes.
  • 5-year window: Pre-2021 data unavailable; some compounds may have earlier emergence.
  • Polysemy: CBD and THC are ambiguous terms that may include non-cannabinoid searches.
  • Low-volume censoring: Queries below Google Trends threshold may show as zero.
  • Regulatory status: Current as of 2026-10-02; subject to change.
  • Temporal association: Correlations between regulatory action and search interest changes do not establish causation.
  • Emergence Score: Qualitative indicator based on relative data; not a statistical prediction model.
  • Single collection and reproducibility: the 5-year weekly series behind the v1.0 values were not retained and the aggregate CSV was withdrawn. The first-mention values cannot be recomputed from the repository; they are reproduced from the recorded summary files (and, for THCH, from the research note) as stated in Data Provenance.
  • Repeated collection: a 2026-10-14/23 re-collection of the same terms observed different first non-zero weeks for some compounds (for example H4CBH, HHBD and THCV). v1.0 keeps the 2026-10-02 record and does not treat the runs as interchangeable; stability is not established.
  • Cohort label: baseline/emerging is a market-status classification, not a first-mention threshold. HHC is emerging although its first non-zero week (2021-11-28) precedes 2022.
