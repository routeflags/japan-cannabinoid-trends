Key Findings

  • 12 cannabinoids tracked in Japanese Google Trends data from 2021 to 2026
  • 6 compounds are now regulated under Japanese law (THC, HHC, THCH, HHCH, THC-O, CBN)
  • HHCH shows clear regulation effect: search interest peaked 3 weeks before regulation, then disappeared
  • H4CBH and HHBD emerged in 2025, approximately 8 months apart
  • 4 new cannabinoids appeared in Japanese search data during 2022-2023

Citable Findings

The following findings are designed for direct citation in research, journalism, and policy documents.

Citation Unit 1

HHCH Regulation Effect

"In Japanese Google Trends data, HHCH (hexahydrocannabihexol) first appeared in the week of 2023-03-26 and peaked at a relative interest score of 100 in the week of 2023-11-12—approximately three weeks before it was designated as a controlled substance on 2023-12-02. Following regulation, search interest dropped to near zero and has remained minimal through 2026."
Source: First-Mention Timeline Research | Accessed: 2026-10-02 | Note: Google Trends values are relative indices, not search volumes.
Citation Unit 2

CBN Regulation Timeline

"CBN (cannabinol) was designated a controlled substance in Japan effective June 1, 2026, following a series of health incidents reported since October 2024. The designation was announced on March 18, 2026, via Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare ordinance."
Source: MHLW Official Page | Accessed: 2026-10-02
Citation Unit 3

H4CBH and HHBD Emergence Sequence

"In Japanese Google Trends data, H4CBH first appeared in the week of 2025-04-06, while HHBD first appeared in the week of 2025-11-30—approximately eight months later. Both compounds are semi-synthetic cannabinoids that emerged during the same market expansion period."
Source: First-Mention Timeline Research | Accessed: 2026-10-02
Citation Unit 4

Regulated Compounds Summary

"As of October 2026, six of twelve tracked cannabinoids in Japan are subject to specific regulations: THC (cannabis control act, 1948), HHC (designated drug, 2022), THCH (designated drug, 2023), HHCH (designated drug, 2023), THC-O (narcotics control act, 2024), and CBN (designated drug, 2026)."
Source: Regulatory Status Summary | Accessed: 2026-10-02
Citation Unit 5

New Compound Emergence Rate

"Between 2022 and 2025, four new cannabinoids appeared in Japanese Google Trends data for the first time: THC-O (March 2022), THCH (April 2022), CRDP (July 2022), and HHCH (March 2023). Two additional semi-synthetic cannabinoids, H4CBH and HHBD, emerged in 2025."
Source: First-Mention Timeline Research | Accessed: 2026-10-02
Citation Unit 6

Regulatory Category Distribution

"Japanese regulation of cannabinoids employs multiple legal frameworks: the Cannabis Control Act for THC and conditional CBD/CBG, the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act for designated drugs (HHC, THCH, HHCH, CBN), and the Narcotics and Psychotropics Control Act for THC-O. This multi-framework approach reflects different risk assessments for different compound classes."
Source: Regulatory Status Summary | Accessed: 2026-10-02

Data Source

Regulatory Sources

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) official sources, including designated drug lists and press releases.

Limitations

  • Relative indices: Google Trends values (0-100) are relative, not absolute search volumes.
  • 5-year window: Pre-2021 data unavailable; some compounds may have earlier emergence.
  • Polysemy: CBD and THC are ambiguous terms that may include non-cannabinoid searches.
  • Low-volume censoring: Queries below Google Trends threshold may show as zero.
  • Regulatory status: Current as of 2026-10-02; subject to change.
