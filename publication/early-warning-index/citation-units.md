## Citation Unit 1: HHCH Regulation Effect

**Finding:** HHCH search interest peaked one month before regulation, then disappeared.

**Citable sentence:**
> In Japanese Google Trends data, HHCH (hexahydrocannabihexol) first appeared in the week of 2023-03-26 and peaked at a relative interest score of 100 in the week of 2023-11-12—approximately three weeks before it was designated as a controlled substance on 2023-12-02. Following regulation, search interest dropped to near zero and has remained minimal through 2026.

**Source URL:** https://github.com/routeflags/japan-cannabinoid-trends/blob/main/research/20261002-first-mention-timeline.md

**Method note:** Google Trends values are relative indices (0-100), not absolute search volumes.

## Citation Unit 6: Regulatory Category Distribution

**Finding:** Regulatory approaches vary by compound type.

**Citable sentence:**
> Japanese regulation of cannabinoids employs multiple legal frameworks: the Cannabis Control Act for THC and conditional CBD/CBG, the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act for designated drugs (HHC, THCH, HHCH, CBN), and the Narcotics and Psychotropics Control Act for THC-O. This multi-framework approach reflects different risk assessments for different compound classes.

**Source URL:** https://github.com/routeflags/japan-cannabinoid-trends/blob/main/research/regulatory-status/SUMMARY.md

**Method note:** Classification based on official Japanese legal sources.

## Limitations

1. **Relative indices:** Google Trends values (0-100) are relative, not absolute search volumes.
2. **5-year window:** Pre-2021 data unavailable; some compounds may have earlier emergence.
3. **Polysemy:** CBD and THC are ambiguous terms that may include non-cannabinoid searches.
4. **Low-volume censoring:** Queries below Google Trends threshold may show as zero.
5. **Regulatory status:** Current as of 2026-10-02; subject to change.
