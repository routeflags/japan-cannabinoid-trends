**Citable sentence:**

**Interpretation:**

## Limitations

1. **Relative indices:** Google Trends values (0-100) are relative, not absolute search volumes.
2. **5-year window:** Pre-2021 data unavailable; some compounds may have earlier emergence.
3. **Polysemy:** CBD and THC are ambiguous terms that may include non-cannabinoid searches.
4. **Low-volume censoring:** Queries below Google Trends threshold may show as zero.
5. **Regulatory status:** Current as of 2026-10-02; subject to change.
6. **Temporal association:** Correlations between regulatory action and search interest changes do not establish causation.
